How an engagement runs

Four stages, in order. Each one has to hold up before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    We learn your company

    We talk to anyone who wants to make their job more efficient at your company and come out knowing how your business actually runs, not how the manual says it does.

  2. 02

    We build a demo

    We go away and build something working, designed around your business's own needs. You see exactly what can come off your desks before you commit to anything.

  3. 03

    We automate

    The demo becomes the real thing, built out and running on your systems, with your keys. Your data never leaves your business.

  4. 04

    We teach your people

    Then we hand it over properly. Your team learns to run it, change it, and build their own — so the thing that outlasts us is what they know, not what we left behind.

Our software prepares. Your people decide.

Nothing that commits money, a truck or a customer moves without one of your people. Choosing the carrier included.

The work we take off your desks is the gathering, the typing and the chasing. The judgement stays where it already sits.

What this looks like in a freight business

Different every time, but the work usually starts in the same few places.

Who we work with

Brokerages and carriers running McLeod, Turvo, Revenova, Trimble, or something built in-house years ago and never replaced.

If your dispatchers and billing clerks spend the day in email, PDFs and copy-paste, that is the work we take on.

Where it usually starts

Rate confirmations and BOLs read and keyed for you. Check calls chased and logged. Invoices matched against what was quoted. Carrier packets and insurance certificates kept current without anyone remembering to look.
