How an engagement runs

Four stages, in order. Each one has to hold up before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    We learn your company

    We talk to anyone who wants to make their job more efficient at your company and come out knowing how your business actually runs, not how the manual says it does.

  2. 02

    We build a demo

    We go away and build something working, designed from your businesses unique needs. You see exactly what can come off your desks before you commit to anything.

  3. 03

    We automate

    The demo becomes the real thing, built out and running on your systems, with your keys. Your data never leaves your business.

  4. 04

    We teach your people

    Then we hand it over properly. Your team learns to run it, change it, and build their own — so the thing that outlasts us is what they know, not what we left behind.
