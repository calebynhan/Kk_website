ABOUT

Good operations people are doing work a computer should do.

We've spent years inside operations teams where the real system is a spreadsheet somebody maintains at 6am and three people who know what to do when it breaks.

The software they get sold is built for an average company that doesn't exist. So they keep the spreadsheet, and the software becomes another thing to update.

We work the other way around: learn one company properly, build for how it runs, then make sure its people can keep going without us. Deliberately small: two of us, a handful of clients a year, nobody to keep billable.

Caleb Han

Co-founder of CollageAI, a generative-AI platform incubated by Morningside Group, which manages over $3B. He shipped its first thirty-odd features and ran the Harvard pilot that lifted student learning gains 62%. Before that he ran technical due diligence on Morningside's $1.5M acquisition of StudyWise.

Palantir awarded him a $10,000 Valley Forge Grant for the work this business is built on: taking raw data, shaping it into an ontology, and putting an autonomous agent on top of it. In an engagement that is what he owns — the data model, and the agents that run on it.

HOW WE WORK
