ABOUT

Good operations people are doing work a computer should do.

Someone reads one screen and types what it says into another. Someone waits on an answer that should have arrived on its own. It's real work, and it fills the day of the person you hired to think.

In a lot of operations teams, the real system is a spreadsheet somebody maintains at 6am and three people who know what to do when it breaks. The software they get sold is built for an average company that doesn't exist. So they keep the spreadsheet, and the software becomes another thing to update.

We work the other way around: learn one company properly, build for how it runs, then make sure its people can keep going without us. We started this year, and there's no bench to keep busy. What we take on is what the two of us can do.

HOW WE WORK

Your systems, your keys.

What we build runs on your infrastructure, under your accounts. Nothing routes through us, and there's no platform to be locked into.

Training is the point.

Training is part of the work, not an upsell. If your team still needs us in a year, we did it badly.

We learn how your company works, build the tooling it's missing, and teach your people to run it.
